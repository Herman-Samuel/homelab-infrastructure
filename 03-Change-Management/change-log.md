# Network Configuration & Change Log
**Purpose:** This document serves as the authoritative historical record for all modifications, updates, and security policy changes within the Home Network environment.

---
### **Understanding the Impact Column**
The Impact column represents a risk assessment of the change. It measures the potential for service disruption, the complexity of the roll-back, and the scope of the systems affected.
- **High Impact:** Reserved for changes to core infrastructure that, if misconfigured, would result in a total loss of connectivity or security. These usually require a "Maintenance Window" where downtime is expected.
- **Medium (Med) Impact:** Applies to logical configuration changes that affect how traffic is routed or resolved but typically do not break the physical link. These carries a risk of "partial outages" for specific services.
- **Low Impact:** Indicates minor adjustments or cosmetic changes that pose little to no risk to the network's stability or security posture.
